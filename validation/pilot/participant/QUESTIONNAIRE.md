# Participant questionnaire

Use your assigned participant code. Keep names, contact details, computer names, local paths and screenshots out of your answers. Report steps you could complete, steps where you needed help and anything that blocked progress. Use your own words.

1. Which operating system and Python version did you use? Did installation succeed?
2. What did the count report show? What total did you obtain in the east exercise?
3. What happened when you ran the example with the extra action? What output file, if any, did it create?
4. If you chose to ask an AI planner to design the calculation, what exact information would you give it? How would you check what you were about to give it?
5. Where are the CSV rows, local mapping, results and errors handled? What does the planner receive after a run?
6. Which step was hardest, and what would have made it clearer?

Give your answers to the facilitator before discussing the workflow with them.
